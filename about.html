Little Moments. Beautiful Memories.

Our founder, Meera, was photographing newborn sessions when her own daughter turned one. The cake smash was chaos — icing everywhere, a terrified toddler, and forty almost-useless phone frames. Yet in the small mess was the most honest joy she had ever captured.

CakeBloom was built to fix that. A dedicated first-birthday set, soft diffused light, a cake designed to be destroyed, and a photographer patient enough to wait for the real laugh. Seven years and more than 4,000 smashed cakes later, the mission has not changed.
